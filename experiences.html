The Elemental Spectrum

Private rituals curated around altitude, geothermal heat, and absolute silence. Select your individual encounters below to shape a bespoke sensory itinerary.

I. THERMAL & WATER
VALS CANTON · PRIVATE SPRINGS

Subterranean Granite Mineral Pools

Heated solely by natural subterranean pressure through fissure networks in the Valser Alps. Private night hours allow solo immersion surrounded by hand-quarried stone and acoustic purity.

Temperature: 38°C – 42°C
Noise: Zero Reverberation
Infusion: Wild Alpine Fir
Private Takeover
II. ROCK & ALTITUDE
DOLOMITI · 2,800M SUMMIT

Dark Sky Geodesic Observatory

Positioned above ninety percent of atmospheric moisture on a sheer limestone terrace. Experience unfiltered deep-space observations through research-grade 16-inch reflectors with resident astronomers.

Elevation: 2,800 m
Atmosphere: Bortle Class 1
Acoustics: High Alpine Wind
Nocturnal Access
III. HEARTH & FIRE
ENGADIN CANTON · WILD EMBERS

The Precipice Open Hearth Feast

Seven courses cooked exclusively over aromatic larch and pine coals upon a cantilevered granite precipice. Paired with aged Swiss vintages matured in rock-hewn cellars.

Heat: Wild Pine Charcoal
Ingredients: 100% Foraged / Local
Cellar: Grand Cru Reserve
Private Chef

HOKKAIDO · OLD-GROWTH CEDAR

Cedar Grove Thermal Meditation

Open-air steaming volcanic springs nestled within ancient cedar woodlands during heavy snowfall. Acoustic meditation bowls vibrate in harmony with the muffled stillness of deep champagne powder.

Sound Floor: < 12 dB
Thermal Contrast: 41°C vs -8°C
Mineral: Volcanic Sulfur
