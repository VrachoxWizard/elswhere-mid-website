Swiss Alps · 45°55' N
DOSSIER REF: EXP-BERNESE-VII

The Bernese Glacier Traverse

A seven-day continuous traverse linking the granite towers of Engadin with the high ice fields of the Jungfrau. Air transfers via private ski-plane allow seamless transitions between uninhabited glacier refuges.

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Total Ascent +4,280 m
Transit Modes Ski-Plane & Heli
Party Size Private (1–4)

EXPEDITION STAGE TIMELINE & WAYPOINTS

01 DAYS 01 – 02

Engadin Base & Thermal Acclimatization

Arrival via helicopter at the Engadin Granite Villa (1,822m). Acclimatization hike and private sommelier pairing dinner.

02 DAYS 03 – 04

Aletsch Glacier Ice Camp

Glacier aircraft landing on the upper ice shelf. Guided crevasse traverse and overnight at the cliffside ice observatory (3,454m).

03 DAYS 05 – 06

The Valais Cantilever

High via ferrata ridge descent into the private Zermatt High Camp with unobstructed views of the Matterhorn north wall.

04 DAY 07

Lucerne Lake Fjord Departure

Scenic low-altitude flight to Lake Lucerne. Farewell vintage cedar speedboat cruise and subterranean cellar celebration.
